# Finding RSS Feeds for Podcasts

## Quick Guide for "岩中花述" (Yan Zhong Hua Shu) Podcast

This podcast is produced by **GIADA** and **JustPod**. Here's how to find the RSS feed:

### Method 1: JustPod Website
1. Visit JustPod's website: https://www.justpod.fm
2. Search for "岩中花述" or navigate to their podcast listings
3. Click on the podcast page
4. Look for RSS feed link (usually labeled "RSS" or "订阅")
5. Copy the RSS feed URL

### Method 2: Podcast Directories
Try these platforms to find the RSS feed:
- **Listen Notes**: https://www.listennotes.com (search for "岩中花述")

### Method 3: Check Podcast Metadata
1. Right-click on the Apple Podcasts page
2. View page source (if available)
3. Search for "rss" or "feed" in the source
4. Look for URLs containing ".rss" or ".xml"

## General RSS Feed Discovery Tips

### Where to Look:
1. **Podcast Website Footer** - Most common location
2. **About/Subscribe Page** - Often has RSS links
3. **Podcast Directories** - Many list RSS feeds
4. **Hosting Platform Pages** - Hosting sites show RSS feeds
5. **Browser Extensions** - Some can detect RSS feeds on pages

### Common RSS Feed Indicators:
- "RSS" or "Feed" links
- Orange RSS icon (📡)
- URLs ending in `.rss`, `.xml`, or `/feed`
- "Subscribe" sections with multiple options

### Testing RSS Feeds:
Once you find a potential RSS feed URL:
1. Copy the URL
2. Paste it in NotePodcast
3. If it works, you'll see the podcast load
4. If you get a CORS error, the feed exists but can't be accessed directly

## Note on Apple Podcasts URLs

Apple Podcasts page URLs (like the one you tried) are **web pages**, not RSS feeds. They:
- Show podcast information in a browser
- Don't provide direct access to audio files
- May have CORS restrictions

**Solution**: Always use the **direct RSS feed URL** instead of Apple Podcasts page URLs.
